# Credits and attribution

EMBER Testing Grounds is an unofficial, non-commercial fan tool for learning to play
**EMBER Obsidian Protocol**. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or supported by the
game's publisher.

## The game

**EMBER Obsidian Protocol** and all of its artwork, card text and rules are
© **Beijing Queti Technology Co., Ltd.** All rights reserved by them.

The card artwork, unit tokens, part illustrations and box covers bundled with this tool are
Queti's work, reproduced here only so players can learn and practise the game. They remain
their copyright and will be removed on request.

The rulebook this tool follows is Queti's free non-commercial online edition.

## The card database

Card data (stats, actions, keywords, points values) and the accompanying artwork were extracted
from the community army builder at **watermelon02.github.io/builder-web**, a fan project by
**watermelon02** (`github.com/watermelon02/builder-web`). That site did the substantial work of
transcribing the whole card catalogue; this tool would not exist without it.

## The mission and battlefield cards

The Main Task, Secondary Task and Battlefield card scans in `assets/missions/`,
`assets/secondary/` and `assets/battlefield/` come from the Korean **namu.wiki** article on
EMBER: Obsidian Protocol, which documents them in detail. namu.wiki content is licensed **CC BY-NC-SA 2.0 KR**, and the cards themselves remain
Queti's copyright. Our English text for those cards is a translation of the printed Korean, so
treat it as unofficial and let the physical card win.

## The demo scenarios

The ten preset scenarios are converted from fan demo booklets by **Luna Lilly Games** (Ryan).
Scenario names, deployments and objectives are theirs.

I found the booklets through **u/Soavon**, who shared them with the EMBER community on Reddit.
Thanks for putting them where people could actually find them; this tool would have shipped
without any scenarios otherwise.

## What is original here

Only the application itself: the board, rules helpers, dice engine, squad tracker, attack and
detonation flows, and the English translations of card text that the source data left
untranslated. Those translations are unofficial. Where confidence was less than high they are
labelled as such in the app, and the original text is always available alongside them, so the
printed card is always the authority.

enjoy it.
